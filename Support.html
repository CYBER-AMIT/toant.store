Frequently Asked Questions (F & Q) 💡

What is crypto mining?
Crypto mining is a process through which new cryptocurrencies are created and transactions are verified.
How can I start mining?
Create an account on our platform and follow our provided guidelines.
How long does payout take?
Payouts are usually processed within 24 hours, but may take longer depending on network conditions.
I'm having trouble connecting my wallet, what should I do?
Ensure your wallet address is correct and check your internet connection. If the problem persists, please contact our support team.
Are there any fees for using the platform?
Small fees may apply for certain services, which are clearly stated on our website.
How can I increase my account security?
Use a strong password and activate two-factor authentication (2FA).
